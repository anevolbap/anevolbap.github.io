* {{{icon-code}}} My packages

- [[https://github.com/anevolbap/pyrobustlm][pylmrob]]: a Python port of the =lmrob= MM-estimator from R's [[https://cran.r-project.org/package=robustbase][robustbase]]. It matches R on the classic validation datasets. On [[https://pypi.org/project/pylmrob/][PyPI]], with [[https://pyrobustlm.readthedocs.io][docs]].

* {{{icon-code}}} Contributions

- [[https://github.com/pymc-labs/CausalPy][CausalPy]] — Bayesian causal inference by PyMC Labs. Collaborator with commit access, [[https://github.com/pymc-labs/CausalPy/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+author%3Aanevolbap][41 merged PRs]]. Some of them: covariates in state-space time series ([[https://github.com/pymc-labs/CausalPy/pull/1111][#1111]]), a placebo-in-time calibration plot ([[https://github.com/pymc-labs/CausalPy/pull/1140][#1140]]), a summary for instrumental variable models ([[https://github.com/pymc-labs/CausalPy/pull/851][#851]]), and mypy as a real CI gate ([[https://github.com/pymc-labs/CausalPy/pull/1145][#1145]]).

[[https://github.com/pymc-labs/pymc-marketing/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+author%3Aanevolbap][49 merged PRs]].
